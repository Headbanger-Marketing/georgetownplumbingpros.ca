FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

Answers to the questions Georgetown homeowners ask us most.

Are you licensed and insured in Halton Hills?

Yes on both counts, and every job needing one is permitted through the Town of Halton Hills and inspected under the Ontario Building Code. The closed-permit paperwork goes home with you.

Do you actually work evenings and weekends?

Yes, as standard scheduling, because most Georgetown households are out all day. After-work weekday slots and Saturdays are first-class bookings here, not overtime bargaining.

How quickly can you replace a water heater?

Common tank sizes are on the truck, so most Georgetown replacements happen same or next day, including old-unit removal, permit, and commissioning.

Our house is from the fifties. Repipe or repair as things fail?

Worth a real inspection first. Scattered pinholes and falling pressure point toward planned replacement, while one isolated issue can be repaired. We assess honestly and quote both paths.

Can you handle well and septic properties around Georgetown?

That is home turf. Rural Halton Hills properties get full well-side service, pressure tanks, iron and sulphur treatment, UV, and septic-line plumbing from a crew that likes the countryside.

Does Georgetown water justify a softener?

Most households notice the difference quickly, since town supply carries a real mineral load and area wells run harder. Your address-specific test makes the answer concrete.

What does a quote cost, and is it firm?

Quotes are free, written, and itemized, and the number holds unless scope genuinely changes, at which point we stop and re-confirm before continuing.

Do you go outside Georgetown itself?

Yes: Glen Williams, Norval, Acton, Limehouse-side rural routes, Milton, and Erin. If your address is on a concession road, mention the nearest intersection when you book.

Do you serve areas outside of Georgetown, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Georgetown and nearby communities including Acton, Norval, Glen Williams, Milton, and the surrounding area.
